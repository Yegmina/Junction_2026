## Why stay with AfterQuery

The work already includes a custom survey world, actions/observations, constrained time and flight resources, image evidence, candidate reports, a hidden evaluator and an MCP adapter. Those are direct parts of an agent-learning environment. The closest alternatives introduce mandatory hardware integrations or a different primary application.

However, physics realism, dataset size and SFT/GRPO execution do not substitute for the required frontier-agent/reference comparison. The main delivery priority is the reproducible judging bundle: a fair stronger reference, three frontier-agent runs, do-nothing and stop-early baselines, reward-exploit checks and a one-command harness run. Smaller-model learning remains supporting/optional work.

## Current decision

Continue with AfterQuery as the main entry. Keep existing Gemini API and Gemma work as potential additional tech-challenge evidence, subject to their own rules. Do not add unrelated sponsor integrations or pivot to a new hardware/data product merely to fit another title. Existing bounded research can finish; primary effort returns to the benchmark.
