# Third-party components and publication

- DuMux 3.10.0 and derived physics application files retain GPL-3.0-or-later
  notices. DUNE modules retain their upstream licences and exceptions.
- Gemma weights retain the model publisher's licence and model card. Do not
  distribute pretrained weights inside the application source archive.
- Frontend/package dependencies retain their published licences.
- User-provided labelled images and flight recordings remain private research
  inputs until their publication rights are established. Synthetic examples
  may be packaged separately with complete generation provenance.
- Credentials, original Codex chat history and cloud identity tokens are never
  part of a source release or challenge evidence package.
