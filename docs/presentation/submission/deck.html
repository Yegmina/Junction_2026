AeroRozum.Reward integrity
Public tools. Private authoritative state.

Reward evidence that survives verification.

0.65 × retrieval F₂
+ 0.20 × region coverage
+ 0.15 × independent views

A report, airborne evidence, home return and flight/action compliance gate the reward.

One-to-one matches penalize duplicate and unsupported claims. Independent views require ≥1m horizontal separation.

A separate simulator owns the ledger

602 training images; 120 development images. The checkpoint was frozen before opening 182 test-zone images. Label provenance and raw generations are retained.
